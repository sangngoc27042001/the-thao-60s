# Privacy Policy

**App name:** Thể thao 60s
**Operator:** Vo Ngoc Sang ("I", "me", "my")
**Contact:** sangngoc27042001@gmail.com
**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Thể thao 60s (the "app") accesses when you connect a TikTok account, how it is used and how you can remove it. The app is a personal tool used to manage my own TikTok account.

## 1. Information the app accesses

When the TikTok account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app may access, depending on the permissions granted:

| Data | TikTok permission (scope) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Display name and avatar | `user.info.basic` | Identify the connected account |
| Profile details such as bio and profile link | `user.info.profile` | Show account information |
| Follower, following, likes and video counts | `user.info.stats` | Report account statistics |
| List of your public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts | `video.list` | Analyze video performance |
| Upload videos or photos to your drafts | `video.upload` | Send content to TikTok for you to finish in the app |
| Publish videos or photos to your account | `video.publish` | Post content you choose to publish |

The app also handles the video and photo files that you choose to upload, and the status that TikTok returns for each upload.

The app does not access your TikTok password, private messages, contacts or payment information.

## 2. How the information is used

The information is used only to:

- show you your own account information and statistics;
- upload or publish the content you choose, after your explicit confirmation;
- keep the connection to your account working (for example, renewing the access token).

I do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, and I do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Storage and retention

- Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the device or server that runs the app, in its configuration, and are not published.
- Account statistics are read on demand and are not kept longer than needed to answer a request.
- Uploaded video and photo files are kept only as long as needed to complete the upload, and then deleted or kept on my own storage.
- I keep no database of other people's data.

## 4. Sharing

Information is shared only with TikTok, as needed to perform the actions you request through the official TikTok APIs. Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

## 5. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect stored tokens, including restricting access to the device that runs the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your choices and deleting data

- You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under **Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions**.
- You can ask me to delete any stored tokens or data related to your account by emailing **sangngoc27042001@gmail.com**. I will do so within 30 days.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

## 8. Third-party services

TikTok processes your data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy). I am not responsible for TikTok's practices.

## 9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
